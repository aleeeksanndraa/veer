The studio

A canal house room with eight oak reformers

Tall windows, lime plaster, no mirrors on the walls. You hear the springs and the teacher, nothing else. Tea and a shower after class.

House rules

Phones sleep in the basket by the door. One hour without a screen is part of the class.

Grip socks on, shoes off. We keep spare pairs at the desk.

On your first visit, come ten minutes early. We show you the reformer before anyone else arrives.

Nobody watches you. Everyone is busy with their own springs.

Questions people ask at the desk

Can I still book through ClassPass?

Yes, a few spots per class. Booking here gets you the intro price and first place on the waitlist.

What if the class is full?

Join the waitlist. If someone cancels, you are booked in automatically and get an email. Free cancellation up to 8 hours before.

Is it OK with back pain or after pregnancy?

Tell your teacher before class. Foundations and Slow are the best start. For an injury, check with your physio first.

Do classes run in English?

Every class shows its language in the timetable. Most run in English, some in Dutch, all teachers speak both.
